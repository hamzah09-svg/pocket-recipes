Pocket Recipes

Quick recipes for busy days

Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Juicy chicken breasts seared until golden, then simmered in a rich, velvety cream sauce loaded with sun-dried tomatoes, fresh spinach, and grated parmesan cheese. It brings a comforting, restaurant-quality Italian flavor right to your table

Spicy Sesame Peanut Noodles

A quick and satisfying dish featuring chewy noodles tossed in a savory, velvety sauce made from peanut butter, soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, and chili crisp. Garnish with crushed peanuts and chopped green onions for the perfect crunch

Honey Garlic Glazed Salmon

Tender salmon fillets pan-seared and coated in a sticky, sweet-and-savory glaze made of honey, minced garlic, soy sauce, and a touch of lemon juice. It is an effortless, nutrient-rich meal that cooks in under 15 minutes
